Our Coaches

Real footballers.
Real coaches.

Art of Football was founded by two real footballers, and real coaches, who bring high-level playing and coaching experience into every session.

Olivier Beltran-Woodward

Co-Founder & Head Coach

Coach Olivier is an experienced footballer and coach with a strong background in the NSW football pathway. Currently playing First Grade for Macarthur Rams, Olivier has also represented Northern Tigers and Dunbar Rovers at First Grade level and has established himself as one of the most prolific attacking players in NPL2.

His playing achievements include being named NPL3 U20 Player of the Year, reflecting his ability to perform at a high level and his understanding of what it takes to develop as a young footballer.

Olivier also brings hands-on coaching experience, having worked with students at The King's School and St Pius X College, alongside his coaching involvement with Coerver Football Academy. Combining high-level playing experience with a passion for developing young players, Olivier is committed to delivering engaging, professional and development-focused football programs in schools and local communities.

Jude Azar

Co-Founder & Head Coach

Coach Jude is a First Grade goalkeeper currently playing for Granville Rage in NPL3, having previously represented Prospect United and Fraser Park at First Grade level. Jude has also developed extensive coaching experience, including coaching at NPL level and working with students at The King's School for the past five years. He has also worked with Kano Football, gaining experience across different football development environments. Jude is currently working towards his AFC C Diploma coaching licence.

Jude brings a unique combination of football and education experience to his coaching. He has worked in education for the past five years across OOSH and learning support, while studying a Primary Education degree at the University of Sydney.

With experience both on the pitch and in the classroom, Jude is passionate about creating positive, structured and engaging football environments that help young players develop their skills, confidence and understanding.
